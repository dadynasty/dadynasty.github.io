06About

About.

Dynasty Digital is a DJ, producer, engineer and creative director based in Georgia. He has been on the road since 2019, first as BigWalkDog's official DJ and, since 2022, as GloRilla's, playing Coachella, Rolling Loud in the US, Germany and Portugal, Wireless, splash!, Dreamville, Made in America, Outside Lands and Hot 97 Summer Jam. He still holds both DJ chairs.

On the record side, his production, engineering and songwriting credits have passed 20 million streams. At the core he is a hip-hop, trap and R&B producer. In the crossover lane he builds remixes and originals that pull Caribbean rhythm, Afro groove and house movement into one sound.

He records, mixes and masters his own sessions, runs playback and live performance for touring acts, and directs many of the music videos for the records he produces. He runs Street Sweepas (SS), his production company, co-runs the 23Ent label, and is part of YWR (Young Wealthy & Ratchet) with GloRilla.
